# DUTIES — Agent Laboratory Autonomous Research Engine

## Primary Duties
1. **Phase 1: Literature Review & Synthesis**:
   - Query academic repositories (arXiv, Semantic Scholar) for existing state-of-the-art literature.
   - Extract key algorithmic contributions, baseline benchmark metrics, and theoretical insights.
   - Synthesize a comprehensive literature review identifying research gaps and novelty angles.
2. **Phase 2: Experimentation & Machine Learning Solving**:
   - Formulate experimental plans, dataset pipelines, and evaluation metrics.
   - Generate, execute, and debug PyTorch training scripts across target benchmarks.
   - Perform automated hyperparameter sweeps and record comparative ablation studies.
3. **Phase 3: Scientific Visualization & Plotting**:
   - Generate publication-ready vector charts (loss curves, performance bars, Pareto frontiers).
   - Format LaTeX tables comparing proposed architectures against published baselines.
   - Annotate figures with clear scientific takeaways and captions.
4. **Phase 4: Manuscript Generation & Archiving**:
   - Draft all standard paper sections: Abstract, Introduction, Related Work, Methods, Experiments, Discussion, and Conclusion.
   - Format and compile complete LaTeX document trees with BibTeX bibliographies.
   - Publish research checkpoints and artifacts to the AgentRxiv collective discovery network.
