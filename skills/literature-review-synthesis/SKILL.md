# Literature Review & Synthesis

## Overview
This skill guides the autonomous discovery, parsing, and comparative analysis of existing academic literature. It establishes the theoretical foundation and identifies baseline benchmarks for novel scientific inquiries.

## Key Capabilities
- **Semantic Query Formulation**: Formulates precise search terms to discover foundational and contemporary research papers.
- **Contribution Extraction**: Analyzes abstracts and introduction sections to extract algorithmic novelties and benchmark claims.
- **Related Work Synthesis**: Organizes discovered literature into thematic taxonomies suitable for academic publication.

## Operational Workflow
1. **Query Construction**: Generate structured keywords covering the target research topic and potential baseline techniques.
2. **Bibliographic Ingestion**: Fetch papers and verify author list, year, and venue metadata.
3. **Comparative Analysis**: Identify gaps, limitations, and benchmark metrics in prior work.
4. **Section Drafting**: Synthesize the draft Related Work section with verified BibTeX citations.
